How do I get a programme?

Have Entrayn build one, start from a plan, or build your own.

  1. On your home screen, tap Get a programme.
  2. Choose one:
    • Entrayn builds it for you makes a programme from your training profile: your goal, days, equipment and any injuries.
    • Start from a plan lets you pick one of our plans.
    • Build my own sets up your days for you to fill in.
  3. Check the preview. Entrayn checks it against your equipment, injuries and goal. If something doesn't fit, tap Swap these for me.
  4. Choose when to start, then tap Start this programme.

Good to know

  • Building takes about 30 seconds.
  • Entrayn building it for you is part of Solo Pro, free while it's in beta. Starting from a plan or building your own is always free.
  • Already have one? Tap Start a new programme on your home screen. It replaces your current one. Everything you've logged stays in your history.
  • A programme isn't medical advice. Stop and get help if anything hurts.
