1. Go to [entrayn.app/signup](https://entrayn.app/signup).
2. Choose **I'm training on my own**.
3. Enter your date of birth. Training on your own is for people 18 and over. We only use it to check, and we don't keep it.
4. Create your account and agree to the terms.
5. Answer the short health check.

## If the health check says to check with your doctor

You'll see **Check with your doctor first**. You can still look around and log, but you can't start a programme until you tap **My doctor has said I can exercise**.

## Good to know

Training on your own is on **Solo Pro**, free while it's in beta.
