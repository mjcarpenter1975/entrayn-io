1. On your home screen, tap **Edit my programme**.
2. Make your changes:
    - **Swap** an exercise for another one
    - change **Sets** and **Reps**
    - **+ Add exercise** or **Remove** one
3. Tap **Save changes**.

## Good to know

- Reps can be a number, a range like 8-10, or a time like 45s.
- If you've already trained this week, removing an exercise waits until next week. Swaps and sets × reps change straight away.
- Anything that doesn't fit your equipment or injuries is marked, so you can swap it.
