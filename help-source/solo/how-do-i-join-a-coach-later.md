## With a coach code

1. Go to **You**, then **Join a coach**.
2. Enter your coach's code and tap **Connect**.
3. Read **What your coach will see**, then agree.

Until your coach accepts, you keep training on your own as normal.

## With an invite

Open the invite link from your coach, sign in, then read what your coach will see and tap to agree and join.

## What changes

- Your training history comes with you, and your coach takes it from there.
- Your plan becomes free while you have a coach.
- Your coach can't see your health check answers, or your progress photos unless you share them.
