Sometimes Entrayn suggests a change on your home screen, based on your sessions and check-ins.

1. Read **Entrayn's suggestion**. Tap to see why.
2. Choose **Got it**, **Change my programme** or **Not for me**.

Your answer helps Entrayn learn what's normal for you.

## When you report pain

If you report pain in your check-in, suggestions pause. Take it seriously:

- **Chest pain, fainting or trouble breathing:** stop exercising and get help straight away.
- **Pain that's sharp or getting worse, swelling, numbness, or pain at rest or at night:** get it checked first.

## Resuming suggestions

When your pain has settled, tap **Resume training suggestions** and answer the questions. It's available from the day after you report pain.

Resuming is your choice. It isn't medical clearance, and Entrayn doesn't give medical advice. If the pain comes back, report it in your check-in and suggestions pause again.
