1. Open your client and go to **Programme**.
2. On today's session, tap **▶ Start session**.
3. Tick each set as your client finishes it. Change the reps or weight first if they were different.
4. When you're done, save the session.

The session is saved to your client's history, just as if they'd logged it.

## Good to know

- To stop early, tap **End session early**.
- If your client has already started the session on their phone, you'll see **Continue session** instead.
