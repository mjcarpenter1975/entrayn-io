1. Go to [entrayn.app/signup](https://entrayn.app/signup).
2. Choose **I'm a coach**. If you also train yourself, choose **Both**.
3. Enter your details and create your account.
4. On your home screen, the **Getting started** card lists the next steps:
    - **Add your brand**
    - **Add a template**
    - **Add your first client**
    - **Send their invite**
    - **Set up payments** (optional)
5. Tap a step to go straight to it. Each one ticks itself off when it's done.

## Good to know

- Coach plans start with a 30-day free trial. You don't need a card to start.
- The card goes away once the first four steps are done. You can also hide it.
- Each step has its own guide: [add your brand](/help/coaches/how-do-i-add-my-brand), [use a template](/help/coaches/how-do-i-start-from-a-template), [add a client](/help/coaches/how-do-i-add-a-client) and [invite a client](/help/coaches/how-do-i-invite-a-client).
