1. On the **Programme** step of adding a client, choose **Entrayn Intelligence**.
2. Under **Describe your client**, write a few lines about their goals, experience and anything to avoid.
3. Generate the programme. It usually takes 20 to 30 seconds.
4. Under **Review before sending**, tap any day to change sets, rest or exercises.
5. Tap **Continue** when you're happy.

## Good to know

- Always review an AI-drafted programme before you send it. You're responsible for making sure it suits your client.
- More detail gives a better draft. Mention injuries, equipment and how long sessions can be.
- If it can't make a programme, try again or use a template.
