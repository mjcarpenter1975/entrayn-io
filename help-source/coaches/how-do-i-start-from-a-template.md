## Use a template for a client

1. On the **Programme** step of adding a client, choose **My templates**.
2. Pick a template and tap **Use this template →**.
3. Change anything you need for this client, then tap **Continue**.

You can also choose **Built-in**, Entrayn's own programme for the goal and split you picked.

## Make a template

- **Copy a starter:** go to **Programmes**, then **My templates** (on a phone, **Library**, then **Templates**). Tap **See starters**, pick one and tap **Copy to My templates**.
- **Build one:** tap **+ New template**.
- **From a client's programme:** open **Edit programme** and tap **Save as template**.

## Good to know

- Saving from a client's programme can copy its notes. Check none are only about that client.
