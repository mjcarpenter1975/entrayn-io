## Change the current programme

1. Open your client and tap **Edit programme**.
2. Change exercises, sets, reps or rest. Hold a tile to move it to another day.
3. Tap **Save changes**.

## Start the next block

1. Open your client and tap **Start next block**.
2. Set it up, then check **Review the new block**.
3. Tap **Activate new block**, then **Activate**.

The current block is put away in their history, and the new one starts.

## Good to know

- Your home screen shows **Block ends this week** when it's time to plan the next one.
- To stop your client swapping exercises, turn on **Lock programme** on their **Overview**.
