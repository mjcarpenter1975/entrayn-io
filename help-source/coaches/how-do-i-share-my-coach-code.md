1. Go to **Settings**, then **Your coach code**.
2. Tap **Copy** and send the code to your client.
3. Your client enters it in their app and sends you a request.
4. On your home screen, find **Connection requests**.
5. Tap **Accept**, or **Decline** if it isn't someone you coach.

## Good to know

- An invite is quicker for someone new. The code suits people who already have an Entrayn account.
