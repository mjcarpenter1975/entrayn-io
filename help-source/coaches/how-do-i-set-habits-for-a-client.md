1. Open your client and find **Habits**.
2. Tap **Add a habit**, or **Start from a suggestion**.
3. Name the habit, and choose **Every day** or a number of **Days a week**.
4. Choose a start date and save.

Your client ticks it off on their home screen, for today or yesterday.

## Reading the week

- Weeks run Monday to Sunday in your client's time zone.
- A daily habit is on target at 5 of 7 days.
- A habit shows in amber only when your client has fallen well short two weeks in a row. Tap **Message** to check in.

## Good to know

- Up to five habits at a time. Most clients do best with one to three.
- Habits are for everyday routines, not medication or medical treatment.
- To change a habit, tap **Change**. Changes start the next day, and past ticks stay as they were.
- To stop one, tap **End habit**. Its history stays.
