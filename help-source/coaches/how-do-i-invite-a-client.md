1. Add your client. See [How do I add a client?](/help/coaches/how-do-i-add-a-client)
2. On the last step, tap **Send invite**. Your client gets an email with a secure link.
3. They open the link, create their account and see their programme.

## If the email doesn't arrive

1. On your clients screen, open the **Invited** list.
2. Find your client and tap **Resend invite**.
3. Or tap **Copy invite link** and send it to them yourself, for example by text.

## Good to know

- Invite links last 7 days. Resending gives a fresh link.
- Ask your client to check their junk folder.
- Each invite is for one email address. If your client signs in with another address, the link won't work for them. See [Why doesn't my invite link work?](/help/faq/why-doesnt-my-invite-link-work)
