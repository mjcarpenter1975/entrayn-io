1. On your clients screen, tap **Add new client**.
2. On **Details**, enter their name and email.
3. Choose how you'd like to start:
    - **Build programme, then send invite** sets up their programme first.
    - **Send invite now, build later** invites them straight away.
4. If you're building first, work through the remaining steps: **Goal**, **Programme**, **Review**, **Health** and **Confirm**.
5. Tap **Send invite**. Your client gets an email with a link to join you.

## Good to know

- Your progress is saved as a draft, so you can stop and come back to it.
- Base plans cover up to 20 clients. Drafts don't count.
- If you use AI to draft the programme, review it before you send it. You're responsible for making sure it suits your client.
