## Someone who has joined you

1. Open the client and go to **Overview**.
2. Scroll down to **Danger zone** and tap **Remove client**.
3. Confirm.

They come off your list straight away, and you lose access to their data. They keep their account and their full training history.

## Someone you invited who hasn't joined

1. On your clients screen, open the **Invited** list.
2. Find them and tap **Remove client**, then confirm.

This deletes them and their draft programme.

## Good to know

- Any habits you set for them end automatically.
- Download your data first if you want a copy. See [How do I download my data?](/help/coaches/how-do-i-download-my-data)
