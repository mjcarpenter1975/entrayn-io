1. Tick a set. Your rest timer starts straight away.
2. With ten seconds to go, a voice says "Ten seconds until next set."
3. You'll hear a short tone at 3, 2 and 1, then a lower tone at zero.

## Turning the sound off

Tap the mute button in the session to mute the voice and the tones. It stays muted until you finish that session.

## Good to know

- The timer only counts down on screen. The sounds carry the last ten seconds.
- If your phone was locked during the countdown, missed sounds aren't played late.
- Can't hear it? See [Why can't I hear the rest timer?](/help/faq/why-cant-i-hear-the-rest-timer)
