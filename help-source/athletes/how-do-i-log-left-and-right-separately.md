Some exercises, such as lunges and split squats, open with a **Per side** label and two lines for each set.

1. Enter the reps and weight for your left side and your right side.
2. Tick the set once. One tick covers both sides.

## For other exercises

Tap **⋯** on the exercise, then **Log left and right**.

## Good to know

- If both sides were the same, tap **Same both sides** to go back to one line.
- Tap the copy arrow under the set number to copy both sides from the set above.
- You can log one side on its own if you need to.
