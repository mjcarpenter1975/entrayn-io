## Download your data

1. Go to **You**, then **Your data**.
2. Tap **Download spreadsheet**.

You get one spreadsheet with your sessions, sets, check-ins, injuries, programmes, messages and your coach's notes about you. **Download for another app (.json)** gives the same data for other apps.
