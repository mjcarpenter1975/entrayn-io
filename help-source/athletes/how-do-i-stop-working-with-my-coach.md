You can't do this in the app yet. Email [hello@entrayn.app](mailto:hello@entrayn.app) from the address you use for Entrayn, and say you'd like to stop working with your coach.

If you're moving to a new coach, tell us who, and we'll help with that too.

## What happens

- Your coach can no longer see your data.
- You keep your account and your full training history.

## Good to know

You may want to download your data first. Go to **You**, then **Your data**.
