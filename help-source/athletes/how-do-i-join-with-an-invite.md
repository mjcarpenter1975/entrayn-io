1. Open the invite email from your coach and tap the link.
2. Choose a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Tap **Create account & view programme**.
4. Read **Before you start**, tick each line, then tap **I agree — let's go →**.
5. Answer a few short questions about your training, so your coach knows you better. You can tap **Skip for now** and finish them later.

Then you'll land on your programme. If your coach is still building it, it shows up there when it's ready, and you'll get an email.

## Already have an Entrayn account?

Sign in with your existing password, then tap **Accept & continue**.

## Good to know

- Invite links last 7 days. If yours has expired, ask your coach for a fresh one.
- The link only works for the email address it was sent to. See [Why doesn't my invite link work?](/help/faq/why-doesnt-my-invite-link-work)
