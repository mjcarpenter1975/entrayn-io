1. Go to **You**, then **Connected apps**.
2. Tap **Connect** next to Withings.
3. Read what Entrayn will receive, then connect.
4. Sign in to Withings and allow access.

Your last 90 days of weigh-ins arrive first, then each new one. They show under **Body** on your Progress tab.

## What Entrayn receives

Weight, body fat %, lean mass and muscle mass, with the date and time of each weigh-in. Your coach can see this to coach you. It is never sold or used for advertising.

## Disconnecting

Tap **Disconnect** under Withings, then **Disconnect and delete**. Entrayn stops receiving your weigh-ins and deletes your Withings data.

## Good to know

Weigh-ins Withings isn't sure are yours, and numbers you type into the Withings app, aren't sent to Entrayn.
