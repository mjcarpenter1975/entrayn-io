You can't delete your account in the app yet. Email [hello@entrayn.app](mailto:hello@entrayn.app) from the address you use for Entrayn, and ask for your account to be deleted. We'll reply to confirm the next steps.

## Before you ask

Download a copy of your data. Go to **You**, then **Your data**, then **Download spreadsheet**.
