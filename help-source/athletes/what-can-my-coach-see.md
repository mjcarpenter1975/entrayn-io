While you're connected to your coach, they can see:

- your programme, sessions and sets, and the notes you write on them
- your check-ins, injuries and pain scores, and your notes to them
- your messages with them
- data from apps you connect, such as Garmin or a Withings scale
- habits they set for you, and your ticks

Your coach can't see:

- your progress photos, unless you choose to share them
- habits you set for yourself

When you stop working with a coach, they lose access straight away. You keep your full training history.
