1. Ask your coach for their coach code.
2. In the app, go to **You**.
3. Under **Connect to a coach**, enter the code and tap **Connect**.
4. Wait for your coach to accept your request.

## Good to know

- Changed your mind? Tap **Cancel request**.
- If your coach sends you an invite instead, use that. See [How do I join with an invite?](/help/athletes/how-do-i-join-with-an-invite)
