1. On the **Programme** tab (your home screen), find today's session and tap **Start session**.
2. If your reps or weight were different from the plan, change them first.
3. Tick each set when it's done. Your rest timer starts by itself.
4. To tell your coach anything, tap **Note** on an exercise.
5. When you've finished, choose how the session felt.
6. Tap **Session complete — save it**.

## Good to know

- You can do your sets in any order. Every set can be ticked on its own.
- If you need to stop early, tap **Not feeling it today — end session early**.
- A group labelled **Superset** shows exercises that belong together.
