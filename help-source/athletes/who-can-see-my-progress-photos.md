1. Go to **Progress**, then **Progress photos**.
2. Tap **Add photos**, then **Take or choose photo** for front, side or back.
3. To let your coach see them, turn on **Share my progress photos with** your coach.

## Good to know

- Photos are stored privately, and location data is removed.
- Your coach only sees them if you turn sharing on.
- You can delete any photo at any time.
- Same spot, same light and same distance each time makes them easier to compare.
