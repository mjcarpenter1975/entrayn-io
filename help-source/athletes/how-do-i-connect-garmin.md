1. Go to **You**, then **Connected apps**.
2. Tap **Connect** next to Garmin.
3. Read what Entrayn will receive, then connect.
4. Sign in to Garmin and allow access.

New workouts and sleep appear each time your watch syncs.

## What Entrayn receives

- Your workouts: type, time, distance, pace, heart rate and elevation
- Your sleep, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability (HRV) and stress
- Your daily activity and fitness, such as steps, intensity minutes and VO2 max

Your coach can see this to coach you. It is never sold or used for advertising.

## Disconnecting

Tap **Disconnect** under Garmin, then **Disconnect and delete**. Entrayn stops receiving your data and deletes your Garmin data.

## Good to know

- Data arrives from the day you connect. Tap **Fetch last 7 days** to fill in recent days.
- Not syncing? Open the Garmin Connect app and let your watch sync, then check again.
