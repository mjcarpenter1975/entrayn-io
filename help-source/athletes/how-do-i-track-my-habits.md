1. On the **Programme** tab, find **Today's habits**.
2. Tap a habit to tick it off.
3. Forgot yesterday? Switch to **Yesterday** and tick it there.

To see all your habits and how your weeks went, tap **See my habits**.

## Good to know

- You can tick today or yesterday only.
- A blank day just means it wasn't logged.
- A week counts once a habit covers all seven days. A daily habit is on target at 5 of 7 days.
