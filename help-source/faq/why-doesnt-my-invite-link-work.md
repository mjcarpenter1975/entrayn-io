| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| This invite link has expired. | Links last 7 days. Ask your coach to send you a fresh one. |
| This invite has already been used. | If that was you, just sign in. |
| This invite link is invalid. | Ask your coach for a new one. |
| This invite link is missing its code. | Open the full link from your email, or ask your coach to send it again. |
| Something went wrong checking your invite. | Wait a moment and try again. |

## It says I'm signed in to a different account

The invite was sent to another email address. Tap **Log out**, then open the link again and sign in with the address it was sent to.
