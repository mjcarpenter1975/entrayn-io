You'll see **This invite is for a different account** when you're signed in with one email address and the invite was sent to another.

1. Tap **Log out**.
2. Open the invite link again.
3. Sign in, or create your account, with the email address the invite was sent to.

If you'd rather use the other address, ask your coach to send a new invite to it.
