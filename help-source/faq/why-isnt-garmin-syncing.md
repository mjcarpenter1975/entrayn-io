1. **Sync your watch.** Open the Garmin Connect app and let your watch sync. Entrayn gets new data after Garmin does.
2. **Wait a few minutes.** Data can take a little while to arrive.
3. **Check the last sync.** In **You**, under **Connected apps**, look at **Last sync** for Garmin.
4. **Past days.** Data starts from the day you connected. Tap **Fetch last 7 days** to ask for recent days. If Garmin hasn't been given permission to share past days, only new data arrives.

Still missing? Email [hello@entrayn.app](mailto:hello@entrayn.app) with the date and what's missing, and we'll look into it.
