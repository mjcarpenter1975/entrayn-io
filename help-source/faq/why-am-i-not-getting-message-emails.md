Entrayn emails you when someone sends you a message, so you know to open the app. The email never includes the message itself.

## Check these

1. **The switch is on.** Athletes: **You**, then **Email me about new messages**. Coaches: **Settings**, then **Notifications**.
2. **Your junk folder.** Mark the email as not junk so the next one arrives.
3. **Your email address is right.** It's the one you sign in with.

## When we don't email

- We email once per conversation every 30 minutes at most.
- We don't email while you're already in the conversation.
