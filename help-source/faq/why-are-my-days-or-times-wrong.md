Entrayn sets your days by your device's time zone. Weeks run Monday to Sunday in that time zone.

1. Go to **You** and look under **Timezone**.
2. If it's wrong, change the time zone in your phone's settings, then open Entrayn again.

## Good to know

- Coaches see each client's days in the client's own time zone, so a coach in another country sees the right day for you.
- Travelling? Your days follow your phone. If something looks wrong after a trip, open Entrayn once your phone has updated.
- Still wrong? Email us with what you see and your time zone.
