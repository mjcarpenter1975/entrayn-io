Your weigh-ins show under **Body** on your Progress tab.

## Weigh-ins that aren't sent

- **Weigh-ins the scale isn't sure are yours,** for example when someone else in your home uses it. Assign them to yourself in the Withings app.
- **Numbers you type into the Withings app.** Only weigh-ins from the scale are sent.

## Good to know

- Deleting a weigh-in in Withings doesn't delete it from Entrayn.
- Still missing? Email [hello@entrayn.app](mailto:hello@entrayn.app) with the date.
