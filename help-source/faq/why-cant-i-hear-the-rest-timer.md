1. **Mute.** In your session, check the rest timer sounds aren't muted. Muting lasts for that session.
2. **Volume.** Turn your phone's volume up.
3. **Silent mode.** On an iPhone, sounds may not play when the phone is on silent. Turn silent mode off.
4. **Bluetooth.** If headphones are connected, the sounds play through them.

## Good to know

- The voice and tones only play in the last ten seconds of your rest.
- Missed sounds aren't played late, for example if your phone was locked.
