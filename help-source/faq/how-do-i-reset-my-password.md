1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and tap **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and tap the link.
4. Enter your **New password** twice and tap **Update password**.

## Good to know

- If it says **Link expired or invalid**, ask for a new link and use it straight away.
- Can't find the email? Check your junk folder.
- Signed in already? You can change your password in **You** (athletes) or **Settings** (coaches).
